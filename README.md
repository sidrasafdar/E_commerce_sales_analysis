# E_commerce_sales_analysis
# Summary
## Overview
This project analyzes a dataset from an e-commerce business to uncover insights into sales, profit, and customer segments. The data contains 9,994 entries across 21 columns, detailing transaction-level information such as product categories, sales, profit, shipping methods, customer segmentation, and geographic details. Python libraries like Pandas, Matplotlib, and Plotly were used to perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) and visualize trends and patterns.

## Objective
The primary goal of the analysis was to identify key drivers of sales and profitability and to provide actionable insights for business decision-making. This included examining sales and profit trends by time (monthly), category, sub-category, and customer segment.

## Key Findings
### Sales Trends:

November had the highest sales among all months, while February showed the lowest.
Technology emerged as the highest revenue-generating category, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.
Sub-categories like Phones, Chairs, and Storage contributed significantly to sales, while Fasteners and Art showed the least.
Profitability Insights:

## Monthly profits:
followed a trend similar to sales, with November and December being the most profitable.
Copiers and Phones were the most profitable sub-categories, while Tables and Bookcases incurred losses.
Among customer segments, Consumers contributed the most to both sales and profit.
Sales-to-Profit Ratio:

The Home Office segment had the best sales-to-profit efficiency, followed by Corporate and Consumer segments.
Segment Analysis:

Consumers contributed the largest share of sales and profit, followed by Corporate and Home Office segments.
## Conclusion
This analysis highlights critical patterns and areas of focus for the e-commerce business:

Emphasis should be placed on expanding the sales of high-performing sub-categories like Phones, Chairs, and Copiers.
Loss-making sub-categories like Tables and Bookcases require reassessment to improve profitability.
Marketing strategies targeting the Consumer segment can further enhance profitability due to their significant contribution to revenue.
